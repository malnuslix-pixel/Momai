# Source directory

## Tier 1: primary sources (can stand alone)
| Body | Use for | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) | Exam timetables, results, grading, circulars | https://www.knec.ac.ke |
| Ministry of Education | Policy, announcements, placement, calendars | https://www.education.go.ke |
| Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) | Curriculum designs, approved materials | https://kicd.ac.ke |
| Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS) | University and college placement, cluster rules, cut-offs | https://www.kuccps.net |
| Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) | Loans and bursaries | https://www.helb.co.ke |
| Teachers Service Commission (TSC) | Teacher recruitment, circulars | https://www.tsc.go.ke |
| Kenya Law / Kenya Gazette | Acts, legal notices | https://new.kenyalaw.org |
| Office of the Data Protection Commissioner | Privacy rules and registration | https://www.odpc.go.ke |

Also count as Tier 1: a signed statement, circular or press release that the body itself publishes, or a verbatim video or transcript of an official's speech.

## Tier 2: established news outlets (good for confirmation and context)
Nation, The Standard, The Star, KBC, Capital FM, Citizen Digital, Kenya News Agency. Use at least two independent outlets for any number or date when no Tier 1 document is available, and say who reported it.

## Tier 3: aggregators, blogs, social posts (never the only source)
Education blogs, SEO "results" sites, WhatsApp forwards, TikTok and X posts. Use only as a lead to find a Tier 1 or Tier 2 source. Many repeat each other and contain errors, so do not cite them for exam rules or dates.

## Rules of thumb
- Prefer the original document over any report about it.
- If two sources disagree, say so, cite both, and look for the official document.
- Note the date you checked each source. Rules and bands change between exam series.
- If you cannot confirm something, leave it out or label it clearly as unconfirmed.
