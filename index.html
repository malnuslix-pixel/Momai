    Exams hub: KPSEA, KJSEA and KCSE

    A plain-language guide to Kenya’s national assessments and how to follow results safely. Always confirm dates with the Ministry of Education and KNEC.

    KPSEA

    Level
    End of Grade 6 (primary)
    2026 status
    Results targeted for January 2027
    Next step
    Moves learners on to Junior School, Grade 7

    KJSEA

    Level
    End of Grade 9 (Junior School)
    2026 status
    Results expected in December 2026. Marking involves 13,484 examiners at 24 centres
    Next step
    Informs Grade 10 and pathway placement

    KCSE

    Level
    End of secondary school
    2026 status
    Results targeted for January 2027. 1,048,355 candidates at 10,693 centres
    Next step
    University, college and TVET placement through KUCCPS

    While you wait for results

    1. Plan around a window, not a single day. Dates are targets.
    2. Agree who in the family will check official channels.
    3. Gather documents the school or KUCCPS may ask for.
    4. Keep revision light and steady, and rest well.

    Check results safely

    Use only official Ministry of Education and KNEC channels. Never pay to see results early. Never share your child’s index number with unknown numbers or links. How to spot fake messages →

    Common questions

    What if results are delayed?

    Release dates are targets. Wait for official updates from the Ministry of Education and KNEC.

    Which exam does my child sit?

    KPSEA at the end of Grade 6, KJSEA at the end of Grade 9 and KCSE at the end of secondary school.

    I received a message offering early results. What should I do?

    Do not reply or pay. Read our explainer on spotting fake messages and report it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do I check KJSEA, KPSEA or KCSE results?

    Only through official Ministry of Education and KNEC channels. Never pay to see results early.

    How is Grade 10 placement decided?

    Placement follows Ministry of Education guidance. Check the Parents’ hub and your school for the current process.

    How do I apply for a HELB loan?

    Apply on the official HELB site, helb.co.ke, and never pay agents to apply for you.

    How do I apply for university or college?

    Placement is coordinated by KUCCPS. Use our cluster-points estimate in Tools, then confirm on the KUCCPS site.

    Are the templates free?

    Yes. Every template is free and downloads as an editable Word file with no sign-up.

    How do I report a mistake or a suspicious message?

    Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form. Report scams to the authorities.

    Grade 10 pathways

    STEM

    Suits learners who
    enjoy experiments, building, mathematics and problem solving.
    Areas of study
    Pure Sciences, Applied Sciences and Technical Studies.
    Possible next steps
    Engineering, health sciences, computing, agriculture and technical trades.
    Ask the school
    Which STEM subjects, laboratories and equipment do you offer?

    Social Sciences

    Suits learners who
    enjoy reading, writing, debate and understanding people and society.
    Areas of study
    Languages and Literature, Humanities and Business Studies.
    Possible next steps
    Law, teaching, journalism, business and public service.
    Ask the school
    Which language and humanities subjects are offered, and in which combinations?

    Arts and Sports

    Suits learners who
    have strong creative or athletic talents.
    Areas of study
    Sports Science, Performing Arts and Visual Arts.
    Possible next steps
    Sports and fitness, music, theatre, design and media.
    Ask the school
    What facilities, coaches and talent programmes do you have?

    Questions to ask at a parent-teacher meeting

    1. How is my child progressing against the expected competencies?
    2. Which subjects or strands need more support at home?
    3. How are projects and classroom assessments recorded?
    4. What can we practise together at home each week?
    5. Which pathway do my child’s strengths suggest, and what evidence supports it?
    6. How can we reach you between meetings?

    Subject combinations vary by school. Follow the Ministry’s placement guidance and try our pathway conversation starter.

    Education jobs

    Vacancies from schools and education organisations. Genuine employers never charge application fees. Every listing is reviewed before it appears.

    Tips for applicants

    • Read the advert fully and note the deadline.
    • Tailor your CV to the role.
    • Prepare certified copies of certificates.
    • Apply only through the contact given in the advert.

    Spot a fake job

    • It asks for an application or training fee.
    • It uses a personal email or unknown number.
    • It promises very high pay for little work.
    • It pressures you to decide quickly.

    Tips for employers

    • State the role, location and deadline.
    • List qualifications and how to apply.
    • Never charge applicants.
    • Give an official contact.
